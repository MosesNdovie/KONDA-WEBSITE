Who we are

Care and connection across generations.

Based in Washington State, Konda YouthNet and Family Health focuses on the needs of underserved, low-income and marginalized communities. Our approach connects health education, family support and pathways to appropriate care.

OUR MISSION

Support wellbeing at every stage of life.

Develop holistic, multi-generational health programs that address health disparities through prevention, education, compassionate support and digital access.

OUR VISION

Equitable, vibrant and healthy communities.

A Washington State where access to healthcare is a foundation for everyone’s wellbeing and where barriers to care are reduced for future generations.

Our program focus

Support built around community needs.

Contact our team to learn about current programs, availability and referral options.

01 / MATERNAL & CHILD HEALTH

Supporting parents and children.

A focus on prenatal and postnatal wellbeing, caregiver education, child development, prevention and connections to appropriate support.

02 / YOUTH-FRIENDLY SUPPORT

Helping young people thrive.

Youth-focused health education, prevention, wellbeing and community engagement, with attention to privacy, trust and cultural understanding.

03 / TELEHEALTH & DIGITAL ACCESS

Connecting people beyond distance.

A focus on virtual-care access and digital literacy to help communities navigate technology and connect with health resources.

04 / SOCIAL NEEDS & FAMILY SUPPORT

Seeing the whole person.

Community outreach, support navigation and referrals that consider social needs, men’s wellbeing and the needs of older adults.
